# FAQ

### **What is ETransfer?**

ETransfer is a cross-chain service developed by the ETransfer team of the aelf community, Etransfer will allow unconstrained transfer of information and value between heterogeneous ecosystems, and push our industry towards more openness and interoperability.

ETransfer is a cross-chain service developed by the ETransfer team of the aelf community. It enables seamless information and value transfer between different blockchain ecosystems, advancing the industry towards greater openness and interoperability. With ETransfer, users can quickly transfer value between different chains.

### **How Secure is ETransfer?**

ETransfer partners with Cobo, a top-tier custody service provider in the industry, for asset management. Cobo custody provides multi-signature wallets, cold storage, and 24/7 monitoring to ensure the safety of digital assets.

### **What makes ETransfer Unique?**

You can check the transfer status in History. If the hash of the receiving chain has not been confirmed yet, please wait patiently for the transaction confirmation. If the hash of the receiving chain has been confirmed and has not yet arrived, you can contact us through support.

Compared to other cross-chain services, ETransfer has the following advantages:

* **low cross-chain fees**

* **Faster**
  * ETransfer ensures faster transaction speeds compared to traditional cross-chain services, allowing users to transfer digital assets in the shortest possible time and significantly enhancing liquidity and efficiency.
* **Secure**
  * ETransfer partners with Cobo, a globally trusted leader in digital asset custody solutions. By using Cobo, ETransfer provides users with high-level security and reliable asset management services.
* **Seamless Integration**
  * ETransfer provides both a Core SDK and a UI SDK, giving developers the flexibility to accommodate a wide range of custom integration requirements.

### **Are there any fees involved?**

For cross-chain transfers, you need to pay two fees:

* **Gas Fee**: Paid by your wallet connected to the From chain for the transfer.
* **Transaction Fee**: Collected by ETransfer in cross-chain tokens to cover the gas fee on the target chain. This fee may vary depending on the network and the level of congestion on that network.

### **What is the difference between the Transfer and Deposit features?**

The specific confirmation time depends on the third-party network. Confirmation on the aelf network is faster. Confirmation usually takes about 4 minutes. You can check the estimated completion time of your transaction on the history page.

ETransfer currently supports two main cross-chain functions: **Transfer** and **Deposit**.

* **Transfer**: Supports cross-chain transfers of a single token between any two chains.
* **Deposit**: Allows users to transfer assets from any chain to the aelf MainChain or aelf dAppChain. It also supports swapping during the cross-chain process.

### **What is the 24-Hour Limit shown on the transfer page?**

The 24-hour limit indicates the maximum amount you can transfer within 24 hours, both individually and in total. This helps manage daily transfer limits for security.

### **Can I connect multiple wallets at once?**

Yes, ETransfer allows you to connect multiple wallets without logging out or switching, making it easy to manage different wallets simultaneously.

### **How do I initiate a cross-chain transfer on ETransfer?**

* Select the “From” chain and “To” chain for the transfer.
* Connect your wallet for the “From” chain and ensure the correct wallet is connected for the “To” chain. Alternatively, you can manually enter the receiving address for the “To” chain.
* Enter the amount you wish to send, and confirm transaction details before pressing “Transfer.”

### **How to deposit to aelf by ETransfer?**

* Connect Portket wallet
* Select deposit asset and receive asset on aelf
* Confirm the chain to be deposited and the chain to receive assets
* Copy the address or scan the QR code to cross-chain deposit

### **How long does it take to complete a cross-chain transfer?**

The confirmation time depends on the third-party network. On the aelf network, confirmation is usually faster, generally taking around 4 minutes. You can check the estimated completion time for your transaction on the history page.

### **What should I do if I fill in the wrong address when transferring?**

If you fill in the wrong address or choose the wrong network, the ETransfer team does not have the ability to help you recover your assets. Therefore, when you withdraw assets, you must check multiple times to ensure that the address and network are correct.

### **What is the ETransfer Telegram bot?**

The ETransfer bot is an official mini-App launched by ETransfer on Telegram. Telegram users can use ETransfer without downloading and registering aelf chain wallet, enabling asset transfers between the aelf chain and other chains. Currently, Telegram users can use all ETransfer functions, including Deposit, Withdraw, and Info.

Go to \[[https://t.me/ETransferOfficial\_bot](https://t.me/ETransferOfficial_bot)] and click "Start" or directly type "/Start" to see the current ETransfer function menu.

### **How do I initiate a withdrawal on ETransfer?**

* Select the “From” chain and “To” chain for the transfer.
* Connect the wallet of the "From" chain and enter the receiving address of the "To" chain.
* Enter the amount you wish to send, and confirm transaction details before pressing “Withdraw”.
